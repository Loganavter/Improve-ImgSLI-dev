## Canvas Navigation

Movement inside the canvas: cursor-centered zoom and middle-button pan.

:::figure{side=block width=280}
![Canvas zoom and pan]({{img:ui.canvas_navigation.zoom}})
Canvas.
:::

### Zoom and pan {#zoom}

- **Zoom** — `Ctrl` + wheel over the canvas.
- **Pan** — middle mouse button + drag.

Comparison canvas techniques live in [Comparison](help://comparison) and [Magnifier](help://magnifier).
