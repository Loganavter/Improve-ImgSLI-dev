## Multi Compare Viewing

Focus one slot, zoom and pan the whole grid, style the labels, and crop per slot.

### Focus mode {#focus-mode}

Click a slot to focus it. A press that turns into a drag moves the slot instead. Click the dimmed area or `Esc` to leave focus. Zoom and pan work inside focus; see [Canvas Navigation](help://view_navigation).

### Zoom and pan {#zoom-and-pan}

- **Zoom and pan together** — zoom and pan apply to the whole grid, not to each slot.
- **Zoom with the wheel** — the point under the cursor stays in place.
- **Stop at the edge** — panning stops at the edge of the image. At fit size or smaller, the view snaps back.
- **Pan with the middle button** — drag with the middle mouse button to pan.
- **Reset** — `0` resets zoom and pan.

### Slot crop {#slot-crop}

Crop trims dark borders of a slot image. Each slot follows the global auto-crop setting unless switched on or off explicitly; the choice shows on screen and in saved images. Empty slots have nothing to crop.
