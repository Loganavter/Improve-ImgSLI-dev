## Multi Compare Viewing

One slot in focus, zoom and pan over the whole grid, label styling, and per-slot crop.

### Focus mode {#focus-mode}

A click on a slot focuses it. A press that turns into a drag moves the slot instead. A click on the dimmed area or `Esc` leaves focus. Zoom and pan work inside focus; see [Canvas Navigation](help://view_navigation).

### Zoom and pan {#zoom-and-pan}

- **Joint zoom and pan** — zoom and pan apply to the whole grid, not to each slot.
- **Wheel zoom** — the point under the cursor stays in place.
- **Edge stop** — panning stops at the edge of the image. At fit size or smaller, the view snaps back.
- **Middle-button pan** — a drag with the middle mouse button pans the view.
- **Reset** — `0` resets zoom and pan.

### Slot crop {#slot-crop}

Crop trims dark borders of a slot image. Each slot follows the global auto-crop setting unless switched on or off explicitly; the choice shows on screen and in saved images. Empty slots have nothing to crop.
