## Triage and Review

Leave one winner per series and move rejects out of the folder.

### Leave one best {#keep-best}

- **{{tr:image_gallery.action.keep_best}}** — marks the best of every series and rejects the rest.
- **{{tr:image_gallery.action.keep_best_desc}}** — marks the best in each series.
- **Show all** — clears every rejected flag.

### Mark best and reject {#make-best-reject}

- **{{tr:image_gallery.card.make_best}}** — marks that image the winner of its series.
- **{{tr:image_gallery.card.reject}}** — toggles the rejected flag on one image.
- **Grid removal** — `Space` / `Delete` take selected cards out of the grid.

### Remove rejected {#clean-review}

- **{{tr:image_gallery.action.clean_rejected}}** — {{tr:image_gallery.action.clean_rejected_desc}} (`_rejected/`). The move is previewed before it runs; files stay on disk.
- **Undo** — press Undo in the notification to return moved files to the folder.

### Check the folder again {#run-triage}

{{tr:image_gallery.action.run_triage}} reopens the current folder and checks it again.

### Choose what to compare against {#set-as-source}

{{tr:image_gallery.action.set_as_source}} and {{tr:image_gallery.card.set_as_source}} choose the reference to compare against. See [Quality Metrics](help://gallery_quality#reference-source).
