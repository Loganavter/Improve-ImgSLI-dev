## Triage and Review

One winner per series, with rejects moved out of the folder.

### One best per series {#keep-best}

- **{{tr:image_gallery.action.keep_best}}** — the best of every series marked, the rest rejected.
- **{{tr:image_gallery.action.keep_best_desc}}** — the best in each series marked.
- **Show all** — every rejected flag cleared.

### Best marks and rejects {#make-best-reject}

- **{{tr:image_gallery.card.make_best}}** — that image marked the winner of its series.
- **{{tr:image_gallery.card.reject}}** — the rejected flag toggled on one image.
- **Grid removal** — `Space` / `Delete` take selected cards out of the grid.

### Rejected removal {#clean-review}

- **{{tr:image_gallery.action.clean_rejected}}** — {{tr:image_gallery.action.clean_rejected_desc}} (`_rejected/`). The move is previewed before it runs; files stay on disk.
- **Undo** — Undo in the notification returns moved files to the folder.

### Folder re-check {#run-triage}

{{tr:image_gallery.action.run_triage}} reopens the current folder and checks it again.

### Comparison reference {#set-as-source}

{{tr:image_gallery.action.set_as_source}} and {{tr:image_gallery.card.set_as_source}} pick the reference to compare against. See [Quality Metrics](help://gallery_quality#reference-source).
